# Recruiting Timelines by Industry (U.S. undergraduates)

Timelines shift year to year and by company; these are typical patterns students report. Always confirm on company career pages and with your campus career center.

## Big 4 and accounting firms (audit, tax, advisory, technology assurance/risk)
- Sophomore year (spring): early-identification / leadership programs (e.g., Deloitte Discovery, PwC Start, EY Launch, KPMG Ignite-style programs). These often lead to junior-year internship offers.
- Junior year (August–October): main internship recruiting. Meet the Firms events, campus career fairs, coffee chats, first-round interviews, then superdays in September–October.
- Internships run in summer (some audit/tax "busy season" internships run January–April).
- Full-time offers are usually extended at the end of the internship; return-offer rates are high. Full-time start dates often fall in the fall/winter after graduation or after the CPA exam.
- Tip: networking (coffee chats, firm events, student organizations like Beta Alpha Psi) strongly influences who gets interviews.

## Management and technology consulting
- MBB and boutique strategy firms: applications for junior-summer internships often open in winter/spring of sophomore year through early junior year; case prep should start months in advance.
- Large consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte Consulting, Booz Allen, Slalom): internship and full-time applications open August–October; some roles continue posting through spring.
- Full-time hiring is heavily from intern classes.

## Investment banking and finance
- Investment banking: very early. Junior-summer internship recruiting can begin in the winter or spring of sophomore year. Networking starts freshman/sophomore year.
- Corporate finance / FP&A rotational programs (e.g., at large corporations): August–October of junior year for internships; senior fall for remaining full-time spots.
- Wealth management: rolling hiring year-round; licensing (SIE, Series 7, Series 66) usually sponsored after hire.

## Technology (software, data, cybersecurity)
- Big tech internships: applications open July–October for the following summer; online assessments soon after applying. Apply early — many roles close once filled.
- New-grad full-time roles: August–November of senior year, with rolling hiring through spring at mid-size companies and startups.
- Startups and smaller firms hire on rolling basis, often 1–3 months before start date.
- Government and defense (cybersecurity): security clearance processes can take months; apply early.

## Marketing, PR, and communications
- Large consumer-goods brand-management programs: August–October.
- Agencies and in-house communications roles: rolling, often 1–3 months before start; spring is the busiest season for summer internships.
- Portfolios and writing samples are commonly requested.

## Human resources
- Corporate HR rotational programs: fall of senior year (some early in junior fall for internships).
- Most HR coordinator and recruiter roles hire on a rolling basis.

## Nursing and healthcare
- New-graduate RN residency programs: applications typically open 3–6 months before graduation; many hospitals have spring and fall cohorts.
- NCLEX licensure must be passed before practicing as an RN.

## Engineering
- Internship and co-op recruiting peaks at fall career fairs (September–October); defense and aerospace firms also hire in spring.
- Full-time offers commonly convert from internships or co-ops.

## General monthly checklist for a junior
- June–July: update resume, LinkedIn, and list target companies.
- August–September: attend career fairs and firm events, network, submit applications.
- September–November: interviews and superdays.
- December–March: second wave of postings; follow up on any waitlists.
- April–May: confirm summer plans, prepare for internship success (return offer).
