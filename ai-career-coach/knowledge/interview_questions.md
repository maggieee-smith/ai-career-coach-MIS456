# Interview Question Bank

This bank is curated from questions commonly reported by students and candidates for entry-level and internship interviews. Groups maintaining this app can add real, recent questions from Glassdoor, Reddit (r/consulting, r/Accounting, r/cscareerquestions, r/FinancialCareers), and campus career center reports in the same format below; the app indexes everything in this folder automatically.

## Universal behavioral questions (all fields)
- Tell me about yourself. (Expect a 60–90 second present–past–future pitch.)
- Why are you interested in this role and this company?
- Tell me about a time you worked on a team and faced a conflict. How did you handle it?
- Describe a time you showed leadership without a formal title.
- Tell me about a time you failed or made a mistake. What did you learn?
- Describe a time you had to manage several competing deadlines.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly.
- Give an example of when you went above and beyond what was expected.
- Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. How did you respond?
- Describe a situation where you had to persuade someone who disagreed with you.
- Tell me about a time you used data to make a decision.
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- What is your greatest strength? Greatest weakness?
- What questions do you have for us? (Always prepare 2–3 thoughtful questions.)

## Consulting / Advisory (Big 4 advisory, risk, technology consulting, management consulting)
- Behavioral: Tell me about a time you had to deal with an ambiguous problem. How did you structure it?
- Behavioral: Describe a time you worked with a difficult client, customer, or stakeholder.
- Behavioral: Tell me about a time you had to explain a complex topic to a non-technical audience.
- Technical/case: A client's profits have declined 20% over two years. How would you figure out why? (profit = revenue − costs framework)
- Technical/case: Estimate the number of coffee shops in Phoenix. (market sizing)
- Technical: What is an internal control? Give an example of a preventive vs. detective control.
- Technical: How would you assess the risks of a company moving its ERP system to the cloud?
- Technical: What is SOX and why does it matter to public companies?
- Firm-style notes: Big 4 first rounds are often behavioral with a recruiter or senior associate; superdays include 2–4 back-to-back interviews with managers and partners, often focused on "why this firm," teamwork, and client service. Management consulting firms (MBB) use full case interviews.

## Technology Assurance / IT Audit / Risk
- What are IT general controls (ITGCs)? Name the main categories. (access to programs and data, program changes, program development, computer operations)
- How would you test whether user access to a financial system is appropriate?
- What is segregation of duties and why is it important?
- What is the difference between a SOC 1 and a SOC 2 report?
- How might AI or automation change the way audits are performed?
- Tell me about a time you found an error or inconsistency in data. What did you do?

## Accounting / Audit / Tax
- Walk me through the three financial statements and how they connect.
- If depreciation increases by $10, walk me through the impact on the three statements (assume a 25% tax rate).
- What is the difference between accrual and cash accounting?
- What is materiality?
- Why audit versus tax (or vice versa)?
- Are you on track for the CPA (150 credit hours)? What is your plan?
- Firm-style notes: accounting interviews are mostly behavioral and fit-focused; technical questions are usually light for interns.

## Finance (FP&A, corporate finance, investment banking, wealth management)
- Walk me through a DCF.
- How do you value a company? (DCF, comparable companies, precedent transactions)
- What is EBITDA and why is it used?
- What is working capital? What happens to cash when inventory increases?
- Pitch me a stock.
- What is the relationship between interest rates and bond prices?
- Why investment banking/FP&A/wealth management specifically?
- Tell me about a time you analyzed financial data to make a recommendation.

## Software Engineering
- Explain the difference between a list and a hash map/dictionary. When would you use each?
- Reverse a string / find duplicates in an array / check if a string is a palindrome. (coding)
- What is Big-O notation? What is the time complexity of binary search?
- Explain REST APIs.
- Describe a project you built. What was the hardest bug and how did you fix it?
- How does Git branching and merging work?
- Firm-style notes: large tech companies typically use an online assessment, then 2–4 technical rounds (data structures and algorithms) and one behavioral round based on company values (e.g., Amazon Leadership Principles).

## Data Science / Analytics / Database
- Write a SQL query to find the top 5 customers by total revenue. (GROUP BY, ORDER BY, LIMIT)
- What is the difference between INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN?
- Explain overfitting and how to prevent it.
- What is the difference between correlation and causation?
- How would you design an A/B test for a new website feature?
- Describe a dashboard you built. Who used it and what decisions did it support?
- What is normalization in a relational database?

## Cybersecurity
- What is the CIA triad?
- Explain the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption.
- What is phishing and how would you train employees to recognize it?
- Walk me through how you would respond to a suspected security incident.
- What is multi-factor authentication and why is it important?

## Marketing / Market Research
- Tell me about a brand whose marketing you admire and why.
- How would you measure the success of a social media campaign? (reach, engagement rate, conversions, CAC, ROI)
- How would you research whether a new product will appeal to college students?
- Describe a campaign or content you created. What were the results?

## Public Relations / Communications
- How would you handle a brand crisis that is trending on social media?
- Pitch me a story about our company to a journalist in 30 seconds.
- What media outlets do you read daily?
- Walk me through a press release you wrote.

## Human Resources
- How would you handle an employee complaint about a manager?
- What makes a great candidate experience in recruiting?
- How do you keep sensitive information confidential?

## Nursing / Healthcare
- Tell me about a time you advocated for a patient.
- How do you prioritize care when several patients need you at once?
- Describe a time you had to communicate with a difficult family member.
- How do you handle a medication error?

## Engineering
- Describe an engineering design project. What tradeoffs did you make?
- Explain Ohm's law and how you would use it to troubleshoot a circuit.
- Tell me about a time a test or prototype failed. What did you do next?
